FTW is the phone app for FTW, open-source home energy software that runs on a box in the user's home (a Raspberry Pi or another Linux computer). The app carries its encrypted app-to-box session through FTW Cloud at cloud.ftw.energy. An optional readable copy in Cloud supports faster views and notifications; the privacy notice explains it. There is no account and nothing to sign in to. You do not have a box, so the app has a built-in demo for you.

THE DEMO
1. Open the app. On the first screen, tap "Try the live demo".
2. The demo runs a simulated FTW box inside the app. It needs no network and no passkey, and it saves nothing.
3. Now shows "Is FTW in control?", live power between solar, battery, home, grid and car, and today's electricity prices.
4. Tap the car to open the car view. Try the level buttons (20/40/60/80 % and ±5), set a goal, then tap Charge now, Pause and Back to the plan. The simulated box answers each command, and the app shows when it was sent, received and done.
5. "Exit demo" at the top returns to the first screen.

WITH A REAL BOX (needs the hardware)
The owner opens the box's local dashboard, chooses Settings → FTW app → Show pairing code and scans the QR code with this app or the iPhone camera (links to https://app.ftw.energy/p). The app then creates a passkey for app.ftw.energy and connects through FTW Cloud at cloud.ftw.energy. The separate relay is retired.

WIDGETS
The price widgets show prices the app saved from a real box. The demo saves nothing, so in review a widget says "Open FTW to see prices".

OTHER
- Privacy policy: "Privacy" at the foot of Now and of the first screen, and in About.
- No account to delete. "Remove this phone" at the foot of Now unpairs the phone.
- Camera: used only to read the pairing QR code.
- No username/password sign-in, no in-app purchases, no ads and no cross-app tracking.
- Cloud holds pseudonymous home and device identifiers. App Privacy declares User ID, Device ID and Other Data as linked to the user.
- The planned project statistics are included in new Cloud terms; local-only users get a separate choice. Household reports have a 48-hour retention limit. Hourly collection remains disabled pending privacy review and policy activation.
- Encryption: Noise protocol built on Apple's CryptoKit only.
- The demo stands in for the hardware, not for a login: the app has no login.
