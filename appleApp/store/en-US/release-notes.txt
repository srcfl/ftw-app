New in 0.6.6: History has an Energy tax refund row for a home in Sweden with a battery. It shows how much grid electricity the battery stored and sent back to the grid in a calendar year, the basis for claiming the energy tax back from Skatteverket, and saves the hours as a file. It needs version 0.158 or later on your box. On the plan's clock the hour after now shows sooner, so 18 no longer goes missing at 17:34. Over a time tomorrow, TOMORROW stands on a line of its own. A battery run under way starts from the level now, as 62 → 26 %, and says it discharges 4.6 kWh.

New in 0.6.5: you can scrub the plan's strip again, under the clock and in the lanes. Drag sideways to read one quarter at a time, or rest your finger a moment. Drag up or down to scroll the page.

New in 0.6.4: price bars start at zero under an even top, so 450 öre against 550 öre looks a fifth dearer, not five times. A dashed line shows the price now. Tap one of the battery's runs or the car's charge in the plan strip on Now, and three figures say when it runs, what it does to the level and the price it meets. The plan is a 12-hour clock with 18:00 straight down, and its ticks show the battery's power. Choose the price the app shows under Home: with or without the grid fee and tax, with or without VAT. The VAT choice needs your box's next update. The car's goal sits at the top of the car view, the whole view scrolls, and a charge that does not go all the way says the goal is still ahead. When your box runs on UTC, the car view shows the goal in the box's time, and with the box's next update the app offers your phone's time zone.

New in 0.6.3: on Now, thin lines of light run between each arc of the ring and the home in the middle. They run in toward the home for power coming into it and out for power leaving it, and faster with more power. Each arc pulses once for every reading from your box. With Reduce Motion on, the ring stands still and an arrow shows the direction. Tapping a button on a notification no longer crashes the app. Tell us if Now feels slow or the phone gets warm.

New in 0.6.2: the plan's lanes show the whole plan your box made, to the last known price, often into tomorrow evening. The rows under them say tomorrow where the time alone would read as tonight. Power charts mark zero and one or two even steps on the scale. When the battery does the same thing twice in a row, the plan says it once.

New in 0.6.1: History scrolls again. Putting its rows in your own order is gone until it can be done without locking the tab.
